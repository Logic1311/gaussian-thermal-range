# General window construction and rational-window corollary
Status: analytic proof for the stated model; full construction not yet machine checked. Not for publication.

Let n=r+1>=2 and let real g_j, 0<=j<n, satisfy sum g_j^2=1 and rho=sum_{j=0}^{n-2}g_j g_{j+1}>0. Put N=2r+5 and theta_l=2*pi*l/N. The two real amplitudes per l are g_j cos(j theta_l)/sqrt(N) and g_j sin(j theta_l)/sqrt(N). Their combined squared modulus is
W_l(k)=(1+2 sum_{d=1}^r C_d cos(d theta_l) cos(d k))/N,
where C_d=sum_{j=0}^{n-1-d}g_j g_{j+d}. This follows by expanding the two squared moduli and using cos A cos B+sin A sin B=cos(A-B), then pairing both signs of i-j. The root-of-unity sum is zero at each nonzero harmonic of absolute value <N. Consequently sum_l W_l=1, and sum_l cos(theta_l) W_l=rho cos(k). Setting T_l=c-2 cos(theta_l)/rho gives sum_l T_l W_l=c-2 cos(k), for every real k. These weights come from actual real amplitudes. T_l>0 for all l iff c>2/rho since l=0 is included.

The normalized circle mean of W_l is 1/N. The discrete mean of T_l^2 is c^2+2/rho^2. The normalized circle mean of D^2 is c^2+2. Expanding the variance and using both normalization identities yields mean sum_l W_l(T_l-D)^2=2(rho^-2-1). The entropy square identity then bounds the mean entropy by 2(rho^-2-1)/(m(c-2/rho)).

For u_j=(j+1)(n-j), let S_n=sum_{i=1}^n i^2(n+1-i)^2. Expand into i^2,i^3,i^4 and use the power-sum formulas (or induct on n) to obtain S_n=n(n+1)(n+2)(n^2+2n+2)/30. Define u_-1=u_n=0. For j=0,...,n, u_j-u_{j-1}=n-2j, including both endpoints. Hence E=sum_{j=0}^n(n-2j)^2=n(n+1)(n+2)/3. The already machine-checked window_energy_identity implies sum u_j u_{j+1}=S_n-E/2. For g=u/sqrt(S_n), rho=1-5/L, L=n^2+2n+2. In particular rho>0 for n>=2. The minimum temperature is m-10/(L-5), strictly positive iff L>5+10/m. The entropy bound becomes 10(2L-5)/(m(L-5)(m(L-5)-10)). If L>=10+20/m then L-5>=L/2 and m(L-5)-10>=mL/2, so this bound is <=80/(m^2 L). Since L=(r+2)^2+1, it is <=80/(m^2 r^2). This is a sufficient explicit threshold, not the exact positivity threshold.

Combining with the already machine-checked physical_entropy_lower gives, analytically, the two-sided order r^-2 for this model. This does not assert a sharp limiting coefficient. For r>=1 the elementary two-bath construction P_+=(1+z)/2, P_-=(1-z)/2, T_+=c-2,T_-=c+2 provides nonemptiness; r=0 cannot realize a nonconstant dispersion. All constants depend on fixed m>0. The original sine window remains a separate tighter construction.
